# Kabbalah

[Play](https://royschwartz.github.io/kabbalah-landscape-study/)

Click or tap the opening screen to advance one line at a time through a short HyperCard-style pixel dissolve. Town passages also appear one sentence at a time. There is no visible Continue button. The complete game view keeps its size, with tiny repeated Malkuth symbols around its four outer edges and Roy’s travel drawing below it at the bottom right. Up/down walk forward/back; left/right turn. Hold a red corner area to combine walking and turning. Keep a finger or the left mouse button held and slide between directions and corners; the pad follows the pointer. The centre and outside the pad pause input, and sliding back resumes the same touch. Held directions light their central diamonds yellow. The centre has no action. A Restart button above the view opens a confirmation before returning to the beginning. Other on-screen game actions are temporarily removed while Roy chooses which to restore.

Hold travel to increase stride distance through ×1, ×2, ×3, ×4 and ×5, spending three completed movements at each stage before increasing and then staying at ×5. While moving forward, left/right steering stays at ¾ of a normal single turn (5.625 degrees), with no turning ramp. Other held turns use ×1, ×2 and ×3. After a completed movement, release adds one final ×1 movement. Keyboard arrows/WASD still walk and turn; Q/E sidestep. Drag the view sideways and release to turn freely. Smoke emerges gently through a narrow chimney outlet, bends with shared eddies and rises through a repeating 192-state physical sequence. Smaller, cooler puffs keep their identity as they drift and thin away. Its naturally varying height stays near twice the former plume; it advances with settled views. A distant size floor keeps it visible in renderer tests at fifteen miles; the current playable map remains smaller. World lighting, controls, clock rules and saved progress remain unchanged.

Typography: Raw regular lettering, with no synthetic bold, shadow or stroke and font smoothing disabled where supported; narrative pixel dissolves remain.
